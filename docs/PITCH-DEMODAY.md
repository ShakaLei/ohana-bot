## 🛡 Q&A ARMOR — the five questions judges always ask
1. **How is it different?** Not a chatbot skin — six real agents with months of shared memory, persistent on-device, bilingual, and it degrades gracefully to pre-rendered audio instead of dying. And it has a deployment date.
2. **What's it cost?** 577–1,580 yuan of parts for the body; the mind runs on a free LLM lane with a \$5 airbag. A school can afford it.
3. **What's next?** Mumbai Nov 3–6 (real deployment), then the body v2 funded by the manufacturing plan; the 3D assembly model IS the build guide.
4. **Who's it for?** Communities: hacker houses, music spaces, schools, festivals — anywhere a shared memory is worth more than a smart speaker.
5. **What breaks?** The LLM lane can stall (→ free-model failover + pre-rendered audio); mic quality in noise (→ push-to-talk + type); the body is unbuilt (→ that's the fundable plan, and the software is done).
