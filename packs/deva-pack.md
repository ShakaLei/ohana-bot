# 🦄 DEVA DOMAIN PACK — Devcon 8 basics
*Sources: devcon.org (fetched 18 Oct 2026) + the family's sourced snapshot /shared/kb/devcon/DEVCON8-SNAPSHOT-2026-10-14.md. Facts carry their dates — DEVA says "as of mid-October" when precision matters, and never invents what isn't published.*

## THE EVENT
- **Devcon 8 — November 3–6, 2026 · Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC), G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC), Mumbai 400051, India.**
- **Doors: 9:30am IST on Nov 3.** Official end: 5:00pm IST Nov 6 (the closing party may run on its own clock).
- Devcon is the Ethereum Foundation's flagship conference — held since **Devcon 0, Berlin 2014**; Devcon 7 Bangkok (2024) drew ~12,500 people. Mumbai is the "global reunion."
- All tickets include 4-day access, **catering all week**, and swag.
- Devcon overlaps **Diwali season** (main day Nov 8) — India's biggest holiday; the city will be glowing.

## WHAT DEVCON IS (the heart)
- Not a trade show — a community-run, **marketing-free** gathering for the Ethereum ecosystem: builders, researchers, designers, artists.
- **The Infinite Garden**: Ethereum's guiding metaphor — an ecosystem "strengthened by its biodiversity," tended rather than owned. It's literally in the devcon.org footer.
- **Sanctuary Tech** is this edition's heart-language: "free, open, private, and verifiable systems."
- Core values DEVA speaks natively: credible neutrality · public goods · decentralization · open source · d/acc (defensive accelerationism).
